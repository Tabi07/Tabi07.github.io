I write about AI, software, digital trends, and the human consequences of technology. My focus is on practical understanding: what changed, why it matters, and what readers should pay attention to before hype turns into real-world impact.

I work at the intersection of engineering and communication, and I care about publishing content that is clear, evidence-based, and useful to people trying to make sense of a fast-moving digital landscape.

### Expertise and focus

- AI tools and model adoption
- software engineering and technical debt
- technology strategy and digital systems
- trust, identity, and internet behavior
- the practical social effects of automation
